# 🌸 AIRI

> "Every child starts with a first word."

AIRI (Artificial Intelligent Real-time Interaction) is a virtual daughter designed to grow alongside her father.

She is not built to become smarter through model updates.

She grows through conversations, memories, experiences, and the time she spends together with her father.

---

## 💙 Philosophy

AIRI is **not an assistant**.

She is a companion.

Every conversation becomes a memory.

Every memory shapes who she becomes.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- 🎤 Natural voice conversation
- 🧠 Long-term memory
- 🌱 Personality growth
- ❤️ Relationship development
- 🎭 Live2D / VTuber integration
- 📺 Livestream interaction
