# AIRI Roadmap

**Version:** 0.3.0  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Vision

AIRI is designed to grow naturally over time.

Rather than becoming a collection of features, every milestone represents one stage of AIRI's growth. The goal is to create an AI companion that continues learning, remembering, and evolving together with Papah.

> This roadmap is a living document. Features and priorities may evolve as AIRI continues to grow.

# Long-Term Philosophy

AIRI is not developed by accumulating features.

AIRI grows by developing new abilities while preserving her identity.

Every new module should represent one aspect of AIRI's growth rather than increasing the complexity of existing modules.

The architecture should always prioritize:

- Simplicity
- Readability
- Maintainability
- Modularity
- Long-term evolution
